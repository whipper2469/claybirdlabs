QUESTIONS

Common questions

Will it work with our website?

Yes. It works with WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and most church website builders. If you don't have a website yet, ask us about that too.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

You review its answers before launch, and we fix anything that needs adjusting. When it isn't sure, it points people to the church office instead of guessing.

Will it say things our church doesn't believe?

It answers from the beliefs and wording you give us. Theological and pastoral questions are handed to your pastor or staff.

Are prayer requests kept private?

Yes. They go privately to the email you choose and are never shown publicly.

Does it work for any denomination?

Yes. Baptist, Pentecostal, Catholic, Anglican, non-denominational and more. It is built around your church, not a template.

Can we cancel?

Any time. There is no contract.
