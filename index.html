WHO WE ARE

Small studio.
Big curiosity.

Clay Bird Labs is an independent software studio based in Kitchener–Waterloo, Ontario. We design and build our own apps end to end — the idea, the interface, the backend, and the support experience.

We like problems that are annoying, specific, and solvable: the kind where a good app saves someone a phone call, a service fee, or an afternoon. We'd rather ship one app people keep than five they delete.

Everything here is built and maintained in-house. When you reach out, you connect with the people behind the product.

Clay Bird Labs at a glance

  • Based in Kitchener–Waterloo, Ontario, Canada
  • Independent and self-funded
  • Native iOS apps and web applications
  • Practical software built around real needs
  • Human support from the people who build it
